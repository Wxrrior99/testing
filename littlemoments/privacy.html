Privacy policy

Little Moments saves your account email, chosen username, memories, dates, moods, tags, favorites, attachments, theme preference, sharing permissions, and reports in Supabase. Email is used for authentication; shared writing displays your username.

Your unshared journal is private from other app accounts. Recipients can read memories you explicitly share. Moderators and admins can review shared memories and reports, hide shared content, and remove shared files. The Supabase project owner has operator access through the dashboard. This service is access-controlled, not end-to-end encrypted.

Use Trash to remove a memory and its attachments. Settings → Delete my data removes your files, memories, shares, submitted reports, profile, and account. Export writing and download attachments first. Admin accounts must have their role removed in the dashboard before account deletion.

Moderation action logs can remain after deletion with account references cleared. Previously downloaded copies and operator backups may remain. Ask the site owner about their backup retention. Google/GitHub process provider sign-in under their own privacy policies.

Operator/contact: the owner of the linked AI Lab Portfolio. Before public use, the operator must add their actual name/contact and retention policy here.
